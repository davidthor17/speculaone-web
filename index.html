The Process

One stay. No warning. Full report.

01

Booking

Our auditor books through the same channels your guests use. No advance contact with the property. No disclosed dates.

02

Stay

A full, paid stay — arrival to departure, every touchpoint a guest would encounter, quietly logged in real time.

03

Report

A shift-by-shift account is compiled: what was met, what was missed, and exactly when. Nothing softened for the file.

04

Seal

Properties that meet the standard carry the Specula mark. Properties that don't, don't — and are told exactly why.
